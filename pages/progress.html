Visual progress summary

Roadmap order and implementation status in one view.

Mycel is still on the narrow first-client path. M2 replay/storage/rebuild remains closed at the current narrow scope, so the active lane centers on M3 / M4. M3 now builds beyond persisted relationship/current-governance summaries, store-index governance context, a deduplicated policy catalog, point-to-point View diff, and copyable profile-reuse hints. M4 now also binds accepted Snapshot/View metadata to fetched objects and applies document-scoped HEADS replace=true behavior on top of its existing production-replication, sequencing, reachability, sender-validation, and negative/warning proof set. Broader governance work and broader session/capability/error-path interop remain open.

  1. Keep M2 closed at the current narrow replay/storage/rebuild scope now that the richer mixed content/metadata competing-branch rebuild/reporting proof is landed.
  2. Expand M3 carefully beyond the current accepted-head reader base, profile discovery/error feedback and reuse hints, governance inspect/list/publish/current workflows, persisted relationship/current-governance summaries, store-index governance context, deduplicated policy catalog, and point-to-point View diff.
  3. Keep M4 narrow while peer-store sync proof grows beyond the current production-replication, sequencing/root gating, advertised Snapshot/View metadata binding, document-scoped HEADS replace=true, reachability, sender-validation, and warning/error baseline.

Milestone Timeline

The roadmap is sequential: each later phase assumes the earlier one is already stable. Right now, the center of gravity is keeping M2 closed at its current narrow scope, the careful expansion of M3, and the remaining M4 interop proof.

This matrix compresses the checklist into section-level status so we can see what is substantively done, what is only partial, and what still belongs to later phases.
